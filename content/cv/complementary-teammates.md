Teammates who complement me best typically have a strong theoretical ML and math background to balance my hands-on SWE/systems experience, creating partnerships where we fill each other's blindspots in techniques, problem solving, and knowledge. I value colleagues with a good eye for avoiding rabbit holes and over-engineering, keeping us focused on effective rather than perfect solutions. Socially, I thrive with approachable people who prefer co-working, enjoy wide-ranging discussions, and are invested in their peers' wellbeing and growth. My most productive collaborations involve teammates who are excited about new approaches and building genuine connections — creating spaces where we can be brutally honest, embrace failure, and explore broader AI safety topics beyond our immediate tasks.
