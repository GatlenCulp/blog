Doing Good Better: How Effective Altruism Can Help You Make a Difference

by William MacAskill

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️

Well written classic of the Effective Altruism community and really effectively lays out the ground work for people getting into the community or those wanting to really do good in their lives. I would definitely recommend people who liked this book to look into other Effective Altruism books. There are a lot of great ones on AI and existential risks and such. The more you dig into it the more philosophy you will uncover and the more interesting and controversial the ideas become.
