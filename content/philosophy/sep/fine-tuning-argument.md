## 01 Fine-Tuning for Life: The Evidence

### 01.01 From Physics

Various physical constants appear precisely calibrated for life, with small deviations producing large effects:

- Gravity strength (without precise tuning: stars/planets/galaxies unable to form, no heavy elements, stars much shorter lived)
- Strong nuclear force (without precise tuning: nothing beyond hydrogen formed, hydrogen all burned)
- Masses of up/down quarks and electron (without precise tuning: instability of proton and neutron)
- Weak Force (without precise tuning: more neutrons, stars couldn't exist)
- Cosmological constant
- Many others...

### 01.02 Evaluating Fine-Tuning Claims

- We may be biased to think of life as we see it today
- We may underestimate life's propensity to appear under different laws/constants/etc.
- Some claim to point-of-view-invariant = Laws are the same in any universe were no special point of view is present(??)
- In multi-dimensional space, changing constants or laws together, there still seems to be a small life-permitting range (Barr, Khan, Tegmark), but others say that the life-permitting range is actually quite large and potentially even more life-friendly (Fred Adams)

## 02 Fine-Tuning for Life: How do we respond?

Fine-Tuning ⇒ Divine Designer or Multiverse assuming probability says this fine-tuning is improbable.

### 02.01 In what sense is fine-tuning improbable

- Physical probability — Says our universe has probability 1 and all others 0 (but if the laws and constants were determined by some random physical process, then this is a bit of a non-useful argument)
- Logical probability — Arbitrary real numbers are possible values of the constants under uniform probability (any other distribution is unfounded). But under this infinite uniform distribution, any finite range has probability 0 (Koperski)
- Why wouldn't conditions be life-friendly?
- Naturalness criterion leads one to expect values for the cosmological constant and mass of higgs particle to be dramatically different from actual ones
- (should probably read this for interpretations on 
probability https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/probability-interpret/)

### 02.02 Does Improbable Fine-tuning Call for a response

- Just because the probability of any outcome of an infinite series of coin flips is 0, we don't have to make some sort of explanation (But people argue that we should just dismiss it as how things happen to be, and I kind of agree. We should strive for a better explanation)
- Some discussion about "causally ramified"
- Talk about how fine-tuning for life fails to take into account the fact that life exists

## 03 Fine-Tuning and Design (Teleological argument)

### 03.01 Divine Fine-Tuning Arguments

Divine Fine-Tuning seems likely even if your priors on a designer are incredibly low.

D = event that designer exists
R = constants are what they are
P+(D) = P(D|R)

### 03.02 Anthropic Principles

- **Weak Anthropic Principle (WAP)**: Our methods of observation are unavoidably biased towards detecting conditions which are compatible with the existence of observers. (Aka Observation-Selection Effects, what we observe must be compatible with the existence of observers)

- **Strong Anthropic Principle (SAP)**: The universe must be such to admit within it the creation of observers at some stage.
  - Only stronger than WAP when conjoined with a version of the multiverse hypothesis.

- **Anthropic Objection**: Basically saying fine-tuning is inadequate because of the anthropic principle (Sober)
  - P(R|D, OSE) > P(R|!D, OSE) is hard to prove.

- **Leslie's Firing Squad**: Prisoner expects to be executed by firing squad, surprised when everyone misses (kind of quantum immortality)
