# My Journey Into AI Safety: From Student to Practitioner

## Introduction

My AI safety journey began at MIT in 2021 when I discovered Effective Altruism[^1] as a freshman seeking career direction. I quickly immersed myself in popular EA/AIS literature[^2] and community engagement through the EA-Intro Fellowship, two EAGs, and an AI safety workshop. During this period, I wrote an article on Transformative AI and later led the EA-Intro Fellowship while managing EA MIT's Office in Spring 2022.

## Early Research Experience

Summer 2022 marked my formal research entry through the SERI Summer Research Fellowship in Berkeley (details in next section). From Fall 2022 through 2024, I served as Operations Director on MIT AI Alignment's (MAIA) executive board, managing organizational strategy, communications infrastructure, and technical problem-solving—occasionally extending to resolving high-stakes administrative issues among members. During this tenure, I completed AI Safety Fundamentals (technical track) and about one week of the ARENA curriculum, attended specialized workshops (two AISST-MAIA technical workshops, one policy-focused, and another hosted in Constellation for university group organizers) and conferences (EAGxLATAM and others), while developing perspectives on AI safety strategy and philosophical/social concerns in light of a radical future.

## Recent Work and Current Focus

Recently, I've transitioned to more applied work: contributing to METR's evaluation infrastructure (Fall 2024), co-presenting on targeted phone-line attacks at CAIP's Congressional Exhibition (February 2025), and collaborating with the MIT Algorithmic Alignment Group on evaluations for AI R&D automation capabilities. Currently, I'm spearheading efforts with MIT Faculty to establish a formal MIT AI Security Institute while maintaining active involvement with MAIA.

## Looking Forward

For the past three years, my career has focused exclusively on mitigating risks from advanced AI, with my current emphasis on technical governance and policy work. I plan to graduate a semester early (Fall 2025) to fully enter this field.
